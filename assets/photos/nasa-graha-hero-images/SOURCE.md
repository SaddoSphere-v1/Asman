# NASA hero photographs of the grahas (Sun, Moon, planets, eclipses)

One iconic photograph per graha for splash/hero panels: Surya (SDO), Chandra (LRO full moon), Mangala (Viking, Valles Marineris), Budha (MESSENGER), Guru (Cassini), Shukra (Mariner 10), Shani (Cassini), Rahu/Ketu (eclipse composites), plus Uranus, Neptune and Earthrise. Downscaled to max 2048px; originals via images.nasa.gov.
