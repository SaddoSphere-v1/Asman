# AstroChart (Kibo) glyph SVGs incl. Hershey stroke sets

- **Source:** https://github.com/Kibo/AstroChart/tree/master/assets
- **License:** MIT (https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)
- **Attribution / credit line:** Matheus Alves (Kibo), AstroChart, MIT
- **Quality tier:** A

Single-stroke (Hershey) glyphs are perfect for plotter-style / engraved looks and animate well with stroke-dashoffset. The upstream files are Inkscape A4 pages with the glyph drawn small; here the root viewBox was rewritten to the content bounding box (path geometry untouched) so they display at full size.
